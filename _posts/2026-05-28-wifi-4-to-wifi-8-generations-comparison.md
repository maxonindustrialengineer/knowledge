## Key Takeaways

- **Wi-Fi 4 (802.11n)** : The foundation, introduced MIMO technology
- **Wi-Fi 5 (802.11ac)** : Focused on 5GHz band, brought wider channels
- **Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax)** : Revolutionized dense environments with OFDMA and TWT
- **Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be)** : Ultra-high throughput with 320MHz channels and MLO
- **Wi-Fi 8 (802.11bn)** : Future standard focused on reliability and deterministic latency
